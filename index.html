SEO findings

Technical and on-page SEO evidence verified against the live site: meta tags, headings, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, canonical behavior and structured data.

S1HighSEO

The sitemap lists 68 URLs, including builder internals and legacy test pages

What we found: sitemap.xml exists (good), but most of its 68 entries are Cargo section partials and old pages: home-hero-section, break, header-(desktop), footer-(mobile)-old, menu-(mobile)-(old), old-design, testing, contact-(old), typeface-(old), old-project-pages and more.

https://gydient.com/home-hero-section
https://gydient.com/break
https://gydient.com/header-(desktop)
https://gydient.com/menu-(mobile)-(old)
https://gydient.com/old-design
https://gydient.com/testing
https://gydient.com/contact-(old)
... 61 more entries

Why it matters

Google is being told to crawl and index fragments that were never meant to be pages. That wastes crawl budget, pollutes the index with thin or broken URLs, and dilutes the authority of the real pages.

How to fix

Remove section partials and legacy pages from the sitemap and mark them noindex (Cargo page settings). Keep only the canonical public pages: home, all-works, about, typeface, news, btc, playground, contact, and the real project case studies.

S2HighSEO

No canonical tags, and www / non-www both serve the site

What we found: no rel="canonical" link exists on any page we tested. Both https://gydient.com and https://www.gydient.com return the full site with a 200 status. The browser console shows Cargo papering over this with a client-side script: "Redirecting to correct domain".

Why it matters

Two live copies of every page split link equity and risk duplicate-content filtering. A JavaScript redirect is weaker than a server-side 301 and is not reliably honored by every crawler.

How to fix

Set the primary domain in Cargo so www 301-redirects to the apex (or vice versa) at the server level, and add a canonical link tag to every page template.

S3HighSEODesktop + Mobile

One meta description for the whole site; homepage title is just "Gydient"

What we found: every page tested (home, all-works, about, contact, dayy) ships the identical meta description: "Tra Giang Nguyen, known as Gydient, is a recognized Creative & Design Director based between Vietnam and Germany." The homepage title tag is only "Gydient", and the contact page title leaks an internal builder name: "Contact D - Gydient" (D = desktop).

Why it matters

Titles and descriptions are what people see in search results. A bare brand name plus a duplicated description gives Google nothing to match queries against and gives searchers no reason to click. "Contact D" looks unprofessional in a results page or browser tab.

How to fix

Write a unique title and description per page. Example for home: "Gydient - Creative & Design Director for Branding, Digital and Campaign". Rename the contact page so the title reads "Contact - Gydient".

S4MediumSEO

No structured data

What we found: no JSON-LD or microdata anywhere on the site.

Why it matters

Person / ProfessionalService schema with sameAs links to Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn helps Google connect the brand entity and can enrich how the site appears in search.

How to fix

Add a JSON-LD block for the person/brand with name, role, location and social profiles.

S5MediumSEO

Duplicate and thin page variants are indexable

What we found: three live contact URLs (/contact, /contact-d, /contact-m), plus /work and /all-works showing near-identical content, all listed in the sitemap.

Why it matters

Duplicate variants compete with each other in the index and split any authority the pages earn.

How to fix

Keep one public URL per page, 301 or noindex the device-specific variants, and drop them from the sitemap.

S6LowSEO

robots.txt blocks third-party SEO crawlers

What we found: robots.txt is valid and references the sitemap (good), but it disallows SemrushBot, MJ12bot, PetalBot, SiteAuditBot and similar.

Why it matters

This is a legitimate choice, but it means backlink and audit tools cannot monitor the site's own SEO health.

How to fix

If outside monitoring is wanted, allow at least one major audit crawler. Otherwise leave as is.

UX findings

Journeys, navigation and content clarity, tested on desktop and mobile.

U1HighUXDesktop + Mobile

The contact page is a dead end

What we found: the Contact page is a single viewport with a large empty black area and one mailto link. There is no form, no location or timezone info, no socials in the body, and no next step. On mobile the tagline "Driven by curiosity, guided by creative excellence." is rendered twice (markers U1). The hero H1 also reads "Gydient@gmail.com" while the footer uses "gydient@gmail.com".

Desktop: the top half of the contact page is empty space (U1).
Mobile: the tagline appears twice on one screen (U1).

Why it matters

This is the page a ready-to-hire client lands on. Every extra bit of friction, or the impression that the page is unfinished, loses real inquiries.

How to fix

Add a short contact form or a booking link, expected response time, location/timezone, and the social links. Remove the duplicated tagline on mobile and standardize the email casing.

U2MediumUX / UIDesktop + Mobile

The sticky filter bar covers project content on All Works

What we found: the All Works / Branding / Digital / Campaign filter bar is sticky and sits on top of project thumbnails and titles while scrolling, on both desktop and mobile (markers U2).

Desktop: the filter pill bar overlaps the project grid (U2).
Mobile: the bar floats over card content while scrolling (U2).

How to fix

Give the sticky bar a solid background and reserve space for it, or make it static below the section header so it never covers content.

U3MediumUX

"More" in the nav goes to a page called BTC, and the menu order changes between devices

What we found: the nav label "More" opens /btc, a page titled "BTC - Gydient" whose heading is "A sneak peek behind the curtain...". Neither the label nor the slug tells visitors what they will get. On mobile the menu lists items in a different order (Work, About, News, Typeface...) than desktop (Work, About, Typeface, News...).

Mobile menu: item order differs from desktop (U3), and the trigger/close control is a plain "+" / "-" text link (see A3).

How to fix

Rename the item to what it is (for example "Behind the Curtain" or "Journal") and keep one consistent menu order across breakpoints.

U4LowUX

The logo links to "/#" instead of home

What we found: the GYDIENT logo points to https://gydient.com/#. It works by accident but appends a hash and is not a real home link.

How to fix

Point the logo at https://gydient.com/.

U5LowUX / Content

The hero statement reads like a broken sentence

What we found: the homepage H1 is "Help brands transform through forward thinking and creative excellence." with no subject, and the same sentence is reused as the hero of the All Works page.

How to fix

Add the subject ("I help brands..." / "We help brands...") and give All Works its own intro so the two pages do not repeat each other.

UI findings

Visual polish, states and rendering issues seen in the live interface.

UI1MediumUIDesktop

The floating nav pill obscures headings while scrolling

What we found: the fixed navigation pill is semi-transparent and sits over section headings such as "Services" as you scroll, leaving text half-hidden behind it (marker UI1).

Desktop: the heading slides under the translucent nav (UI1).

How to fix

Add scroll-margin-top to sections and/or give the nav a fully opaque background so content never shows through it.

UI2MediumUI / PerformanceDesktop + Mobile

Some Selected Works thumbnails render as black boxes

What we found: the "Undiscovered" and "Ventuz" cards show as near-black empty rectangles on load, on desktop and mobile, until their media buffers (markers UI2). Thumbnails are autoplaying videos and animated GIFs with no poster frame.

Desktop: two cards in the Selected Works grid appear empty (UI2).
Mobile: the same blank card problem in the works list.

Why it matters

The work grid is the heart of a portfolio. Cards that look broken undersell the projects, and heavy autoplay media slows the first load.

How to fix

Set a poster frame or static image per card, compress or replace GIFs with muted looping MP4/WebM at lower bitrate, and lazy-load media below the fold.

UI3MediumUIDesktop

An unloaded grey placeholder block on Playground

What we found: the Playground page shows a large empty grey rectangle where the first piece of content should be, with no loading state or fallback.

Desktop: a blank grey block under the intro copy.

How to fix

Check the embed or asset for that block, and add a poster/fallback so a failed load never renders as a grey void.

UI4LowUI

Muted grey headings sit at the edge of readable contrast

What we found: section headers like "Selected Works" and "Selected Clients" use rgb(108,109,101) on black, a measured contrast of 4.0:1. That passes WCAG AA only because the text is large; at smaller sizes the same grey would fail.

How to fix

Brighten the muted grey to at least #9a9a94 (about 5.9:1) so it stays readable at any size and on dimmer screens.

Accessibility findings

What assistive technology and crawlers actually receive from the page.

A1HighAccessibility / SEO

Every image on the site has no alt text

What we found: all 12 images found on the homepage (project covers, thumbnails, logos) have no alt attribute at all. The videos have no captions or text fallback. Cargo renders page content inside shadow DOM, which makes the missing alt text even harder to notice from the editor.

Homepage media inventory (live DOM):
- 12 images, 12 without alt attributes
- 3 autoplaying videos, no captions or transcripts
- Project cards are anchor links whose only content is unlabeled media

Why it matters

Screen readers announce nothing for the entire visual portfolio. Google Images cannot index the work. For a design portfolio, the imagery is the content, and right now it is invisible to both.

How to fix

Set descriptive alt text on every image in the Cargo media settings ("DAYY brand identity - animated logo", "IMAGINE campaign poster"), and give project cards a text label.

A2MediumAccessibility / SEO

Six to seven H1 headings per page, including an empty one

What we found: the homepage has 6 H1s ("Help brands transform...", "Selected Works", "Services", "Branding / Digital / Campaign", "Selected Clients", "Get in touch at..."). About has 7, one of them completely empty. Every page ends with an H1 that reads "Get in touch at Gydient@gmail.com".

Why it matters

Headings are how screen-reader users navigate and how search engines understand page structure. Six top-level headings (one of them an email address) means there is effectively no outline.

How to fix

Keep one H1 per page (the page's actual topic), demote section titles to H2, service items to H3, delete the empty heading, and render the footer contact line as styled text, not a heading.

A3MediumAccessibilityMobile

The mobile menu trigger is a "+" character

What we found: the menu opens from a heading element containing the text "+", implemented as a link to an internal builder page (/menu-(mobile)-1) rather than a button. Screen readers announce it as "plus" or "link, plus", with no hint that it opens navigation (marker A3).

How to fix

Use a button with an accessible name ("Open menu" / aria-expanded state). Keep the visual "+" if you like, but label the control.

What already works well

Worth keeping exactly as is while the fixes above land.

Strong art direction. The black canvas, oversized type and project imagery are distinctive and consistent across pages.
Clean responsive behavior. No horizontal overflow at 390px; grids stack sensibly on mobile.
Working dual-timezone clocks. Berlin and Ho Chi Minh City times were both accurate during testing - a nice trust signal.
robots.txt and sitemap.xml exist and robots.txt correctly references the sitemap.
HTTPS everywhere. http:// redirects to the secure apex domain.
Social and contact links work. Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and the mailto link all resolve.
Favicon, Open Graph image and Twitter card are configured, so shared links render with artwork.
Excellent primary text contrast. Body and headline text measures 19.9:1 on black.
Real project depth. Case-study pages like DAYY are long-form and substantial - the raw material for great SEO is already there.
Desktop home: hero, live clocks and nav render cleanly.
Mobile home: no overflow, type scales well.

Method

How this audit was produced.

  • Live-site inspection in Chrome at 1280x720 (desktop) and a 390x844 iPhone emulation (mobile) on September 18, 2026.
  • Pages tested: Home, All Works, About, DAYY case study, Contact, Contact D, News, Typeface, Playground, BTC, plus sitemap.xml and robots.txt.
  • DOM and accessibility-tree inspection (including shadow DOM, where Cargo renders content), computed-style contrast measurements, console logs, and media inventory.
  • All screenshots in this report are unedited captures from the live site, with numbered markers added to point at specific findings.
  • Scope note: no server access and no lab performance tooling (Lighthouse) was used; performance comments are based on observable media and rendering behavior only.
